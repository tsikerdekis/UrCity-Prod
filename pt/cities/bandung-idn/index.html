Riscos Naturais

Bandung enfrenta os seguintes riscos naturais:

Terremoto Risco Extremo

Located in a highly active seismic zone with frequent major earthquakes.

Vulcão Risco Extremo

Part of the Pacific Ring of Fire with numerous active volcanoes.

Tsunami Risco Extremo

Archipelago frequently affected by tsunamis.

Inundação Risco Alto

Monsoon floods and Jakarta sinking.

Incêndio Florestal Risco Alto

Forest and peat fires during dry season.

Deslizamento Risco Alto

Mountainous terrain with rain-induced landslides.

Onda de Calor Risco Alto

Extreme summer heat.

Seca Risco Moderado

Periodic drought conditions during El Niño.

Tornado Risco Moderado

Indonesia experiences tornadoes.
